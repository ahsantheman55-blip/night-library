      How to use

      Starting

      The Menu is at the bottom of the screen, under its own heading. Press Play. Night Library builds an eight-hour random playlist from every subject and starts reading in its own British voice. VoiceOver does not need to read anything, so you can leave the phone alone.

      Overnight

      Plug the phone in. While it plays, Night Library keeps the screen awake, so leave the app open and do not lock the phone. The iPhone may stop the reading voice if the screen locks or you switch to another app. If that happens, open Night Library again and press Play or Resume where I stopped. It remembers the article and paragraph.

      Tip: VoiceOver's screen curtain, a triple tap with three fingers, turns the display dark while the screen stays awake.

      Choosing what you hear

      Shelves are quick presets, such as a history encyclopedia or a science encyclopedia. Build a playlist gives you tick boxes for subjects and eras, and choices for mode, length and the share of lighter pieces.

      Across time mode plays linked pieces from very different eras that share a theme, such as migration, empire or climate. Before each linked piece you hear a short bridge, for example: from the first farmers forward to today's food supply.

      While listening

      Next and Previous move between articles. Back and Forward one paragraph move within one. What's playing tells you the title, subject and time left. More like this and Less like this change how often similar pieces are chosen. The sleep timer stops the reading after a time you choose, or at the end of the article.

      Reading with VoiceOver

      In Browse, the Read button shows an article as text with headings, so you can read it with VoiceOver at your own pace.

      Keyboard: Space plays or pauses, N next article, P previous, left and right arrows move a paragraph.
